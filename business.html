Business

A small company still deserves a real network.

Fieldline is for offices that have outgrown a single router and a shared spreadsheet of passwords. We design what is there, replace what is failing, and write it down.

What the work usually includes.

Not every site needs all of this. The scope says which pieces are in.

Firewall and switching
A named edge, VLANs that match how you work, and switches that are not a daisy chain under a desk.
Wi-Fi
A survey, not a guess. Staff, guests, and devices that should never see the file server.
Identity
Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace cleaned up: MFA, leavers removed, admins separate from everyday mail.
Endpoints
A patch habit and a way to find a laptop that left with someone. We do not invent a help desk you did not ask for.
Backup
Mail, files, and the one server in the closet. We restore something before we leave.
The room itself
Cabling, a small rack if you need one, power, and a photo so the next visit starts informed.

Project, or ongoing care.

You can hire the build without hiring a retainer.

A project

Walkthrough, written price, scheduled build, handoff. Right when the network is the problem and you want it finished.

Care, after

Monitoring and a person who already knows the site. Offered only when the documentation is good enough that we are not guessing at 7 a.m.

A good fit

Studios, clinics, professional offices, shops with a back room, and warehouses small enough that we can see the whole network in a day. Fifteen people or fifty, if the closet is honest.

Not the fit

We are not a phone company, a web agency, or a 24-hour security desk. If the job is a data center or a multi-state rollout, we will say that early.

Tell us what keeps falling over.

Wi-Fi, the server, the new suite, or the person who used to know all of it and left.
