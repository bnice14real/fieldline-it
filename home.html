Business Home Approach Book a walkthrough

Home

A house is a network. Most of them were never designed.

The router lives in a cabinet because the jack was there. The office drops calls. The cameras share a password with the children. We fix the layout, not the slogans on the box.

Dead rooms

A walk-through with a measurement, then the fewest access points that cover the house. Not a puck in every outlet.

The home office

A cable if we can get one. A work network that does not carry the television, the guests, or the doorbell.

Photos and taxes

A backup you can explain to someone else, and a restore we perform once so it is not theoretical.

Cameras and locks

On their own network. Local storage when you want it. No account shared with the whole family.

New builds

A prewire before the drywall: office, access-point locations, a real closet, and a path for the ISP.

Remodels

We look at the walls that are already open. That week is the cheap week to pull cable.

Renting. We will not open walls you do not own. Placement, one good access point, a wired desk if a jack exists, and a backup that fits on a shelf.

What you leave with.

A map
Where the equipment is, what the network names mean, and which password is for guests.
A restore
One file brought back, so backup is a fact.
A next step
Only if something still needs doing. We do not invent a smart-home project.

Tell us which room drops first.

A floor, an apartment, or a house with a shop in the back. The form is enough.
